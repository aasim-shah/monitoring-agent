# M12 Alerts, Incidents, Notifications Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Real conditions on a customer's servers become tenant-scoped incidents. Each incident is correlated through the asset graph and delivered by email, Slack, or a signed webhook, with a 30-minute cooldown and escalation after 15 minutes. An operator acknowledges and resolves incidents in the dashboard.

**Architecture:**
- **Rules.** Rules are stored per product and come from templates; users never write PromQL.
- **Metric rules** compile into one vmalert group per tenant (`tenant-<orgId>.yaml`). The group carries `extra_label=tenant_id=<org>` (D17). It is written to a volume that vmalert reads, and vmalert is reloaded after each change.
- **Alert path.** vmalert posts firing and resolved alerts to the internal Alertmanager-compatible receiver. The worker evaluates non-metric rules (agent heartbeat missing, connection degraded) every minute and feeds the same incident engine.
- **Incident engine** (`packages/alerting`):
  - deduplicates by fingerprint and reopens within 30 minutes
  - correlates assets and their impact
  - records a timeline
  - queues notifications unless the cooldown applies
- **Delivery.** The worker delivers notifications: email over SMTP, a Slack webhook, or a signed generic webhook. Each delivery gets 5 attempts, then goes to the dead-letter state.
- **Escalation.** The worker's per-tenant tick escalates critical incidents that stay unacknowledged.

**Tech Stack:** Prisma 6 and PostgreSQL 17 (RLS), vmalert v1.126.0, NestJS 11, BullMQ, nodemailer, Next.js 15, Playwright.

**Spec:** spec §13.1, §13.2, §13.4, Scenario B; master plan M12.1–M12.4, D17, §3 alerting (evaluation 1 min, cooldown 30 min, escalation 15 min); WB-13.

**Deferred:**
- **Deployment-related rules and `change_events`.** No change source exists until M7 (GitHub); incidents show "No recent changes recorded".
- **Website availability rules** (website probes are not built yet).
- **On-call schedules, PagerDuty, and Opsgenie** (post-pilot, WB-13).
- **AI summaries** (M14).

## Global Constraints

- **Real conditions only.** Alerts come only from actual conditions; there are no synthetic incidents (BR-13-01).
- **Tenant isolation.**
  - A tenant's rules evaluate only that tenant's series: every group has `params.extra_label: ["tenant_id=<orgId>"]`, and product-scoped rules add a `product_id` matcher (BR-13-06).
  - The receiver accepts an alert only when its `rule_id` belongs to the `tenant_id` label's organization.
- **Fingerprint:** `sha256(tenantId + ruleId + sorted label pairs)`. Volatile labels (`alertgroup`, `alertname`) are excluded.
- **Lifecycle:** open → acknowledged → resolved. A resolved incident whose fingerprint fires again within 30 minutes reopens.
- **Notifications:**
  - Cooldown 30 minutes per incident; it suppresses notifications, never timeline events (BR-13-04).
  - Critical incidents unacknowledged for 15 minutes escalate once to the escalation channels (BR-13-05).
  - Deliveries get 5 attempts with exponential backoff from 10 seconds, then go to dead letter. A Slack or webhook channel that dead-letters 3 times in a row is marked `broken`.
- **Webhook signature:** `X-PCP-Signature: t=<unix>,v1=<hex HMAC-SHA256(secret, "<t>.<raw body>")>`. The secret is shown once and stored encrypted (`encryptSecret`, AAD `notification-channel:<id>`).
- **Wording:** deterministic summaries say "correlated with", never "caused by" (BR-13-03).
- **Receiver access:** `POST /internal/alertmanager/api/v2/alerts` requires `X-Internal-Token` (internal guard) and is never routed by Caddy (BR-13-08).
- **Webhook destinations:** Slack and webhook URLs must be HTTPS and pass `assertPublicDestination` (SSRF).
- **Permissions:**

  | Action | Permission |
  |---|---|
  | View rules and incidents | `incident.view` (all roles) |
  | Manage rules | `alert.manage` (operator, admin, owner) |
  | Acknowledge, resolve, postmortem | `incident.manage` (operator, admin, owner) |
  | Channels | `notification.manage` (admin, owner) |

  Missing permissions are added to the role matrix if absent.

## Review Focus

1. **Tenant A's rule meets tenant B's series with the same metric and agent-like labels.** Expected: it never fires. A forged receiver post with A's `rule_id` and B's `tenant_id` is refused. Covered in Tasks 2 and 4.
2. **A flapping alert (fire, resolve, fire within 10 minutes, 6 times).** Expected: one incident, reopened, one notification, and every transition in the timeline. Covered in Task 3.
3. **A Slack webhook URL pointing at `http://169.254.169.254/` or `https://10.0.0.5/`.** Expected: refused at creation and at delivery, and never fetched. Covered in Tasks 4 and 5.
4. **vmalert down or the receiver unreachable.** Expected: metric dashboards keep working; vmalert retries; no incident is invented. Covered in Task 6 (vmalert's own retry) and Task 8 (the e2e only counts real firings).
5. **An agent revoked or deleted while its heartbeat rule is enabled.** Expected: no "heartbeat missing" alert for a revoked agent, and no crash in the tick. Covered in Task 5.

### Task 2: Rule templates and the vmalert compiler

**Files:** `packages/alerting/src/{templates.ts,compiler.ts}` + `compiler.test.ts` + `vmalert.int.test.ts` (Testcontainers VictoriaMetrics plus vmalert).

**Interfaces:**

- `TEMPLATES: Record<TemplateId, Template>`, where `Template { id, label, kind: 'metric' | 'heartbeat' | 'connection', params: ParamSpec[], defaultSeverity, expr?(p): string, forMinutes(p) }`:

  | Template | Kind | Params (defaults) | Expression |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `host_cpu_high` | metric | `threshold` 90 %, `forMinutes` 10 | `100 * (1 - avg by (agent_id) (system_cpu_utilization_ratio{state="idle"})) > <t>` |
  | `host_memory_high` | metric | 90, 10 | `100 * avg by (agent_id) (system_memory_utilization_ratio{state="used"}) > <t>` |
  | `host_disk_full` | metric | 90, 5 | `100 * max by (agent_id, mountpoint) (system_filesystem_utilization_ratio) > <t>` |
  | `log_error_spike` | metric | `perMinute` 10, `forMinutes` 5 | `sum by (agent_id, service) (increase_pure({__name__=~"log_lines(_total)?",level="error"}[5m])) / 5 > <t>` |
  | `agent_heartbeat_missing` | heartbeat | `minutes` 5 | — |
  | `connection_degraded` | connection | `minutes` 15 | — |

- `RECOMMENDED: TemplateId[]`: all except `connection_degraded`, which is included only when the product has connections.
- `compileTenant(orgId, rules: CompiledRuleInput[]) → string` produces vmalert YAML: one group `tenant-<orgId>`, `interval: 1m`, `params.extra_label: ["tenant_id=<orgId>"]`, and labels `tenant_id`.
  - Each metric rule becomes `alert: <template>-<ruleId8>` with `expr` (a product rule adds `product_id="<id>"` to every selector through the compiler's matcher injection), `for: <n>m`, and labels `{ rule_id, severity }`.
  - Annotations: `summary` is the rule name. `value` is `{{ $value }}`.
  - Non-metric and disabled rules are omitted, and an empty group list produces `groups: []`.
- `validateParams(template, params) → Params` clamps values: thresholds 1–100, perMinute 1–100000, minutes 1–1440. Unknown params are rejected.

### Task 3: Incident engine

**Files:** `packages/alerting/src/{engine.ts,fingerprint.ts,summary.ts}` + `engine.int.test.ts` (database Testcontainers).

**Interfaces:**

- `ingest(tx, ctx, alerts: NormalizedAlert[], now) → IngestResult { opened, updated, resolved, reopened, notify: NotifyRequest[] }`, where `NormalizedAlert { ruleId, labels: Record<string,string>, status: 'firing'|'resolved', startsAt, endsAt?, value? }`.
- **Behavior:**
  - **Firing:**
    - When an unresolved incident with the same fingerprint exists, it gets a `refired` event only if it was last seen more than 5 minutes ago (no event storm).
    - When a resolved incident with the same fingerprint closed less than 30 minutes ago, it is reopened (`reopened`, status open).
    - Otherwise a new incident is created: the title comes from the rule name plus the host name (from `agent_id` → agent hostname), the summary from `summary.ts`, and a `fired` event is recorded.
  - **Resolved:** an open or acknowledged incident becomes resolved with a `resolved` event (note "Condition cleared").
  - **Notify:** opened and reopened incidents produce `notify` requests unless `last_notified_at` is less than 30 minutes ago. In that case a `suppressed` event is recorded. A resolved incident notifies only if it had notified before.
- `correlate(tx, incident)` finds the host asset by `agent_id` (asset identity `agent:<id>`) and then `impact(assetId, depth 4)`. It stores `{ assets: [{id,name,impact}], counts }` in `labels.correlation`.
- `summarize(rule, labels, correlation, value)` returns a deterministic summary, for example "CPU above 90 % on vps-1 for 10 min (now 97 %). Correlated with 3 assets: Orders API (confirmed), …". It never contains "caused".

- [ ] Failing tests:
  - TC-13-03: a duplicate firing produces one incident
  - TC-13-04: a resolved alert resolves the incident
  - TC-13-05: within the cooldown there is no notify request but there is a `suppressed` event
  - Review Focus #2: a flapping alert yields one incident, 6 reopen/resolve pairs, and 1 notify
  - a reopen after 31 minutes creates a new incident
  - TC-13-07: the summary contains "Correlated with" and never "caused"
  - two organizations with the same labels produce separate incidents

  Implement, pass, commit.

### Task 4: API (rules, receiver, incidents, channels)

**Files:** `apps/api/src/modules/alerting/{alerting.module,rules.controller,rules.service,receiver.controller,incidents.controller,incidents.service,channels.controller,channels.service,rule-files.ts}.ts` + `alerting.int.test.ts`, `apps/api/src/config.ts` (`ALERT_RULES_DIR`, `VMALERT_URL`), and OpenAPI.

**Interfaces** (WB-13 §7 routes):

- **Rules:**
  - `GET/POST /api/v1/products/:id/alert-rules`, with body `{ template, name?, params?, severity?, enabled? }`.
  - `POST /api/v1/products/:id/alert-rules/recommended` creates the missing recommended rules (idempotent).
  - `PATCH/DELETE /api/v1/alert-rules/:id`.
  - Every change rewrites `<ALERT_RULES_DIR>/tenant-<org>.yaml` atomically (write a temporary file, then rename) and calls `POST <VMALERT_URL>/-/reload`. A reload failure is logged and does not fail the request: vmalert also re-reads the file on its interval (`-configCheckInterval=1m`).
  - The rule change is audited.
  - When the first non-metric rule is created, a job definition `alert-tick` (60 s, entity = organization) is upserted.
- **Receiver:** `POST /internal/alertmanager/api/v2/alerts` takes the Alertmanager v2 body (an array of `{labels, annotations, startsAt, endsAt, generatorURL}`).
  - Each alert is grouped by `tenant_id`. The org is resolved by `rule_id` through the SECURITY DEFINER function `pcp_resolve_alert_rule_org(rule)`; on a mismatch the alert is dropped and logged (Review Focus #1).
  - `ingest` runs in a system tenant context, notify requests are enqueued to `notification-deliver`, and the endpoint answers 200.
- **Incidents:**
  - `GET /api/v1/products/:id/incidents?status=` returns up to 100 incidents, newest first.
  - `GET /api/v1/incidents/:id` returns `{ incident, events, correlation, sourceLinks }`. `sourceLinks` points to the server page and the service charts.
  - `POST /api/v1/incidents/:id/acknowledge`, `POST /api/v1/incidents/:id/resolve` (with `{note}`), and `PATCH /api/v1/incidents/:id/postmortem` (with `{summary, causeCategory, followUps[]}`).
- **Channels:**
  - `GET/POST /api/v1/organizations/:orgId/notification-channels`. Email takes `{recipients[] ≤ 10}`; Slack takes `{webhookUrl}`, which is stored as the encrypted secret; webhook takes `{url}`, and the API generates a secret returned once.
  - `PATCH/DELETE /api/v1/notification-channels/:id`.
  - `POST /api/v1/notification-channels/:id/test` enqueues a `test` delivery.
  - Every URL must be HTTPS and pass `assertPublicDestination`; otherwise the API answers `400 destination_blocked`.

### Task 5: Worker (delivery, non-metric rules, escalation)

**Files:** `packages/notifications/src/{render.ts,email.ts,slack.ts,webhook.ts,sign.ts}` + tests, `packages/jobs/src/{queues.ts,processors.ts}` (kinds `notification-deliver` and `alert-tick`), `apps/worker/src/config.ts` (`SMTP_URL`, `MAIL_FROM`, `APP_ORIGIN`), and `docs/integrations/webhook-signature.md`.

**Interfaces:**

- **`render(event, incident, origin)`** returns `{ subject, text, html, slack, json }`:
  - The subject is `[CRITICAL] CPU above 90 % on vps-1 — opened`.
  - The body has the summary, a link to the incident, and the correlation counts.
  - The JSON is `{ type: 'incident.opened', incident: {...} }`.
- **`sign(secret, body, t) → header`** and **`verify(secret, body, header, now, toleranceS=300) → boolean`**, matching the documented algorithm.
- **`deliver` processor:**
  1. Load the delivery and its channel.
  2. Send: email through nodemailer (timeouts of 10 s); Slack as a POST of `{text}`; webhook as a POST of JSON with the `X-PCP-Signature` header. Both HTTP sends use the SSRF-pinned client.
  3. Mark the delivery `sent`, or throw so BullMQ retries.
  4. On the final failure, mark it `dead`, increment `consecutive_failures`, and set the channel to `broken` at 3.
  5. A success resets the failure counter.
- **`alert-tick` processor** (per organization, every 60 s):
  - `agent_heartbeat_missing` fires for active agents whose `last_heartbeat_at` is older than N minutes. Revoked or deleted agents are never included (Review Focus #5).
  - `connection_degraded` fires for connections in `degraded`, `error`, or `permission_denied` for longer than N minutes, using `status_changed_at` or `updated_at`.
  - Conditions that are no longer true send `resolved`, through the same `ingest`.
  - **Escalation:** critical incidents open, unacknowledged, older than 15 minutes, and with `escalated_at` null are marked escalated (with an `escalated` event) and delivered to channels where `escalation=true`, or to all channels if none are marked.

- [ ] Failing tests:
  - TC-13-08: the documented algorithm verifies a delivered signature, and a stale `t` is rejected
  - a webhook that returns 500 five times is marked dead and the channel `broken` on the third dead delivery
  - TC-13-02 variant: a heartbeat older than 5 minutes opens an incident, and it resolves after a new heartbeat
  - a revoked agent is never alerted
  - TC-13-06: a critical incident unacknowledged for 15 minutes is escalated once
  - a delivery to a private IP is refused
